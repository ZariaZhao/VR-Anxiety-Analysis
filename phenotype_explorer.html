VR Speech Anxiety Analysis

Research Question
Can physiological signals from brief VR exposure predict speech anxiety — and do distinct response profiles exist?
Data
20 participants × 4 emotional VR scenarios (Russell's Circumplex Model)
Heart rate, speech rate, voice stability + self-report
Key Finding
Heart rate signals explain 64.2% of model predictive power. HR transition B→C is the strongest single biomarker (22.7%)
Contribution
30% of participants show physiological anxiety without self-reporting it — objective monitoring captures what questionnaires miss
💡
Context-induced HR change, not static heart rate, is the dominant anxiety signal. The transition from depressing to tense scenario (B→C) reveals physiological adaptability — a more informative marker than resting HR or personality questionnaires alone. This supports low-cost, wearable-based anxiety screening without self-report dependency.
